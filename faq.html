Our age appraisal combines high-resolution micro-bore cellular dendrochronology, transverse ring-width index matching against Cascadia regional botanical databases, and multi-point acoustic resonance wave velocity analysis.

This multi-tiered protocol delivers botanical species identification with 100% taxonomic certainty and chronological tree age determination with a calibrated margin of error within ±5 to 10 calendar years. For historical salvage lots exceeding 300 years, accelerated mass spectrometry (AMS) radiocarbon validation is performed at our certified testing facility.

Every slab shipped from the VNTimber atelier undergoes an intensive multi-stage dehumidification cycle combining gentle air seasoning followed by temperature-monitored vacuum kilning. We guarantee an Equilibrium Moisture Content (EMC) strictly between 6% and 8% across the entirety of the core and sapwood.

Prior to crating, a pinless electromagnetic dielectric mapping scan tests 24 distinct grid points on both faces. We affix a digital moisture verification seal to each slab’s end-grain certifying uniform fiber stability, virtually eliminating twisting, cupping, or checking after installation in climate-controlled environments.

Restoration processes include organic solvent wax stripping, traditional French hand-polishing with dewaxed shellac, non-toxic micro-crystalline wax patination, and our proprietary Breathable Ceramic Timber Sealant designed for high-traffic dining and conference tables.

Monumental bookmatched slabs and live-edge timbers are individually encased in custom-built, heat-treated birch plywood skeletons featuring shock-absorbing neoprene supports and moisture-impermeable barrier films containing silica vapor-phase regulators.

Shipments move exclusively through dedicated air-ride suspension logistics networks. For residential estates and penthouse installations, our logistics desk arranges site surveys, lift equipment, crane rigging permits, and inside white-glove placement supervised by an accredited timber technician.

We do not participate in clear-cutting or unmanaged extraction. Over 65% of our rare old-growth offerings originate from natural storm falls, civic municipal salvage, or century-old industrial river logs preserved in cold anaerobic sediment.
