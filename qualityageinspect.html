Frequently Clarified Inquiries

Testing Protocols & Scientific Integrity

How invasive is core sample extraction?

For museum-grade antiques and irreplaceable framing, we utilize 1.2mm Swedish increment borer needles or non-destructive sonic frequency tomography. The microscopic insertion aperture is sealed with custom botanical resin matching the timber's natural grain patina.

Can dendrochronology distinguish between red and white oak?

Yes. Cellular anatomy under 200x polarized light clearly distinguishes the closed tyloses in White Oak (Quercus alba) from the open, porous vessels of Red Oak (Quercus rubra), accompanied by genetic marker confirmation.

Are VNTimber certificates accepted by insurance appraisers?

All reports conform to USPAP (Uniform Standards of Professional Appraisal Practice) and are indexed in our public cryptographic registry, ensuring immediate acceptance by international underwriters and customs officers.

What is the difference between radiocarbon and dendrochronology?

Dendrochronology aligns annual growth ring patterns with regional master tree calendars for absolute calendar-year pinpointing. C-14 isotopic decay analysis verifies harvest periods when rings are fragmented or unanchored.
